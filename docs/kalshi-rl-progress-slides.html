What the agents taught us

The newest failure mode is endgame convexity.

The graph showed agents chugging along, then collapsing at the live edge. The saved generations were still positive; the collapse came from current open exposure being marked near binary certainty.

The live edge is not just another historical point. It includes current open positions marked against the latest binary market price.

Current diagnosis

The agents learned mid-window trading before they learned the final minute.

What looks good

  • Recent saved generations still had positive best PnL.
  • Agents are entering trades and creating useful training data.
  • Lineages are differentiating enough to compare behavior.

What is fragile

  • Open exposure can be highly correlated.
  • The wrong side collapses quickly near settlement.
  • Current rewards do not yet isolate endgame discipline.

This is a good learning problem, not just a bug to patch away. The next design should make endgame behavior learnable.

Next experiment family

Let lineages specialize in endgame styles.

Closer Exits early unless edge is huge.
Sprinter Trades only in the last 2 minutes.
Hedger Cuts exposure when price crosses against it.
Conviction Holds through close only with strong signal.
Scalper Closes before the final 60 seconds.

The intent is not to hard-code one answer. It is to let different risk personalities compete under the same paper-only scoring regime.

Next build steps

The next version should measure the endgame directly.

Split the PnL graph

Show realized PnL separately from live marked PnL so a current open shock does not obscure history.

Add endgame features

Seconds to close, target distance, distance velocity, quote acceleration, and price buckets.

Reward discipline

Penalize wrong-side exposure near close and reward profitable late conviction only when it wins.

Protect diversity

Run endgame lineages without deleting existing strong families, Molly, or baseline genetic lines.

Bottom line: the infrastructure is now good enough that the interesting problem is policy behavior.
